How to pair

  1. Choose a network your phone can reach.
  2. Scan its QR code with the phone camera and open DrishtiGuide.
  3. If the camera cannot open it, copy the pairing link to your phone and tap Paste pairing link in the app.

The QR code and link contain your access token. For your privacy, do not share the screen.
